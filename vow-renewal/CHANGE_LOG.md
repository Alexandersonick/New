## Biggest changes (the grovel)

1. **He repairs the public injury in the way Thea chooses (audit E06).** In Ch12 Nolan asks what she wants done about the ninety guests. In Ch13 she decides: he writes a handwritten letter to every guest, with no names and no excuses, saying *"Thea ended the ceremony, and she was right to."* He pays Dimitra her hourly rate for the guest list. In Ch15 eleven letters go to people at his own school, and he dreads the week. In Ch16 Mrs. Haskell has the letter under the glass on her hardware counter, with his permission. In Ch17 a colleague who was at the renewal reacts on the page: *"Well. He signed it."*
2. **He sets the boundary with Leona himself (audit E03).** In Ch12 he speaks first, in front of the group: her number is blocked and he's giving up his chair at the Thursday Table. Ch18 is his last Thursday. In Ch23 he arranges the farewell himself through Abel and states the standing rules: hello-and-keep-walking, he tells Thea the same night, he uses the other pharmacy, and on the anniversaries (22 Oct, 9 Feb) he calls his counselor, Gus or his wife, never her. Leona agrees to keep her end.
3. **He finds his own support.** He sees a counselor in Holland on Mondays. He found her himself and Thea never gets the name (*"I need one thing you can't make better"*).
4. **He tells a truth before she can find it.** In Ch14 he discloses the 6:14 AM call on the morning of the renewal, which is in the call log rather than the message thread.
5. **The search lapse has a consequence (Ch19–22).** He names the danger himself (*"I don't get credit for coming back"*). Margit takes his keys and his Tuesday drive with the kids for a week, and he agrees: next time, keys go to someone before he gets in the car. This pays off when he puts his keys on the table on Christmas Eve.
6. **He asks for forgiveness unprompted.** In Ch27 he asks, kneeling. She answers *"Not all at once,"* and gives the first piece.

## The middle tightened (audit E04)

- **Ch7:** The vow lecture and the "I'm fine" reflection are cut back.
- **Ch11:** Rewritten and about 1,000 words shorter. The casserole, obituary and thank-you-note inventory is gone, so the chapter reaches *"afraid of— the answer"* much sooner.
- **Ch12:** The room setup is trimmed. The new scene work is Nolan initiating the boundary and the conversation about the ninety guests.
- **Ch16:** The duplicate stay-in-the-chair test is cut. A new buffet scene adds ordinary pleasure: a shared roll, Esther's terrible tuna, and Thea's full laugh.
- **Ch17:** The heels-and-exit motif is varied. He swallows *"He's fine"* instead.
- **Ch18:** Rewritten and about 650 words shorter. The car-function explanation is halved. The new pressure: he has given up the only room he had, so the kitchen will have to become that room.
- **Ch19:** The relapse now has a specific trigger. Dara emails that she lands on the 28th and will otherwise take Callum to Lisbon, and Thea fears the box will leave before Margit ever says goodbye. In Ch25 Margit phones Dara herself.
