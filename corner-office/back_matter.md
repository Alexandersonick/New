## A Note to Readers

Thank you for reading *My CEO Husband Gave Her My Corner Office*.

If you enjoyed it, the most helpful thing you can do for an independent author is to leave an honest review wherever you bought this book. A line or two is plenty. Reviews are how new readers find stories like this one, and they mean more than you'd think.

If you'd like to hear when the next book is out, search for Shawn J Dean on Amazon, open my author page, and tap Follow.

Thank you for spending your time with Pip and Adam, and with a room ten by twelve with no post in the corner.

Shawn J Dean
