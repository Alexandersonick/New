### one_star_review_draft (≈185 words, reader register)

## what_they_could_not_complain_about

- **The hook and genre arrive on page 1.** The crane heist opens the book, and the "Twenty hours earlier" humiliation scene follows in Ch2. "You barely use it" lands at the 4% mark.
- **There is no cheating.** It is stated plainly on the page: "I have not touched him, and he has not touched me" (Ch9). Adam ends the 1 a.m. calls himself and tells Simone first (Ch22).

He kneels to apologize (Ch25). His answer to why he gave the room away is the best reveal in the book: "I gave it away so I'd stop having to [look at it]" (Ch25).

- **The voice is specific and funny.** Highlights: Elvin and the dollar lease, the watered ficus, Tyler and the cow, Biscuit Lot, "it's a wall that gave up," the bread-bag rug, the Bedpans.
