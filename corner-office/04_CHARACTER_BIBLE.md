## Philippa "Pip" Sobczak — heroine, POV (first person, present tense), 38

- **Role:** co-founder and chief designer of Twodesk. Owns 33% (Adam 34%, investors 33%).
- **Origin:** West Side Grand Rapids, Polish parish, the Falcons hall, a bowling league.
  Youngest of three, the only girl. Her father **Stan Sobczak** (tool-and-die maker, 41 years
  at a furniture plant) died twenty months before page one. He helped her build Unit 001 in
  his garage in 2014 and built the desk inside it.
- **Want (external):** keep the Clyde Park plant and its crew working, and keep her corner
  honest (the post-less glass corner is the product).
- **Need (internal):** to say what she wants out loud, before it has turned into a grievance.
- **Flaw she owns:** she punishes with competence and silence. She let the office stand empty
  as an accusation instead of telling Adam she cannot sit at her dead father's desk alone.
  She stopped asking him for anything, so he stopped being asked.
- **Competence that colours every scene (job-eye rule):** she reads load paths, how a thing
  carries weight and where it will crack, in rooms and in people. She notices fasteners,
  sightlines, who stands where.
- **What she never says:** "I'm hurt." She says "That's interesting," or makes a joke.
- **Deflection habit:** a joke that answers a different question.

## Adam Kerrigan — hero, POV (first person, present tense), 40

- **Role:** co-founder and CEO. The talker. He raised $310M on the strength of her product and
  his voice. He opened a second plant in Nashville over her objection; it loses money.
- **Origin:** Kalamazoo. His father left with debts when Adam was eleven, and Adam watched his
  mother's paycheck get garnished. Payroll is the one fear he has never said out loud.
- **The lie:** he missed Stan's funeral because he stayed in Singapore one extra day to close
  the Series D. The lead investor would have walked. He told Pip the flight was cancelled. He
  told Simone the truth, at one in the morning, on a call, eight months ago.
- **Error (what must change):** he decides shared things alone. He thinks protection means not
  telling. He took his fear and shame to someone who was not his wife.
- **Voice device:** a salesman who catches himself pitching: "Strike that." He talks to the
  reader the way he talks to a room, and then corrects it.
- **What he never says:** "I don't know." Until he does.
- **Deflection habit:** reframing. He answers a question by making it a better question.

## Simone Achterberg — the "work wife", 46

- VP of Operations, hired eight months ago from a national retail chain's supply-chain group to
  make Twodesk saleable. Divorced, with a daughter at Michigan State. Precise, dry, decent, and
  good at listening at one in the morning.
- She asked for the corner office and he said yes before she finished the sentence. She never
  touched him. She would have liked to be asked, and knows he would only ask her for things
  because she is not his.
- **Not a villain.** She owns the most quotable lines in her scenes. She refuses to keep his
  secret (Ch 21), resigns, and tells Pip. This is the triangle's real cost to him.
- **Idiolect:** complete sentences, no contractions under pressure, numbers only when asked.
