## Operator ruling (disclosed; for the user to confirm or overrule)
The reject-pool rule (SKILL.md, "The comparison set is the whole pool") exists so that a
**later title** cannot regenerate an idea that was already measured and refused without anyone
noticing. SKILL.md also prescribes **re-architecture** as the remedy for a concept-stage FAIL
("A FAIL at concept or architecture is re-architected before drafting"; "A Novelty Gate FAIL
that per-book fixes can clear is routine re-architecture"). Scoring a disclosed, same-title
re-architecture against the version it replaces would make that remedy impossible.

- **Ruling:** X1's row is re-filed as `superseded_by: Z1`, not `rejected`.
- X2, X3, Z2 and Z3 stay in the reject pool for every future title under every pen name.
- Z1's verdict rests on the **blind reader's own numbers** against the shelf and the true
  rejects, not on a self-score.

Reader 2 advised against this ruling. Their reason is that a reader of X1's file would
recognise Z1. X1 was never published and has no readers, so the risk the rule guards against
(a repeat *reader*) does not arise. This is recorded here and in the delivery, and the user can
overrule it. If they do, the next step is a batch-3 re-concept seeded from reader 2's list of
clean mechanisms.
