## Questions for the author (no fix applied)

1. **Ch2 / Ch9, the last time Gus saw Danny.** At the walk-through, "the last time Gus had seen him Danny had been twelve". Gus saw Danny two days earlier at the Supper, handing out tickets. Ch9 says "before this October", but the Supper seems to fall in late September (Sunday after the Supper, then Tuesday, then the Thursday Oct 1 walk-through). Reword, e.g. "the last time Gus had really looked at him"?
2. **Ch19, Dot.** "You haven't ridden in ten years." / "Eleven. I haven't ridden since Arlo." Arlo died in 2020, which is six years ago. Did she stop riding when Arlo stopped, not when he died? Or should the numbers change?
3. **Ch29 vs Ch1/3/6/10/17, Ed's tenure.** Everywhere else Ed has done the Corn for "thirty years", and in Ch17 he said the line to Gus at five, around 1999. In Ch29 he has been there "since Reagan's second term" (1987–89, about 38 years). Which is it?
4. **Ch22 vs Ch26, the barn's east door opening.** In Ch22 Gus says it has been "a sheet of plywood since the end of summer". In Ch26 it has been "covered with a sheet of OSB" for thirty years, and Ruth says Pete wanted a door there "for thirty years". Pete died 11 years ago (Ch7) and poured the floor in 1998, so he could have wanted a door for about 17 years at most. Please confirm Pete's death year and pick one cover story.
5. **Ch26, timing.** Danny arrives at 7:30. Hanging the door takes two hours and painting the sign 1 hr 10 min, which brings us to about 10:50. Mitch is called at "ten-fifteen" and Ruth at 11:20. Should Mitch's call be "eleven"?
6. **Ch25 / Ch27, the trolley.** "Parked the trolley in the middle of the pond on the night of the withdrawal, fifteen days ago." But Ch10 (the night before the withdrawal) already has it parked mid-pond "after Saturday's last ride". Fine if "fifteen days" means since it last ran; flagging in case you want "since the night of the withdrawal".
7. **Ch23, Hank.** Hank "climbed down off Marguerite for the second time in Roz's memory". He was also off the tractor for the hitch pin in Ch13. Ch19's version has the qualifier "between rides"; add it here too?
8. **Ch10, Kenzie.** She is 24 and "rode in seventh grade" (around 2014–15) and threw up crying. Bev then cites this as proof the Lantern Bride (2020 onward) has staying power. Kenzie would have been about 18 in 2020. Intentional (Bev conflating) or a slip?
9. **Ch6, Joyce.** "She had come back for a third night", but we only see her on night 2. Did she ride night 1 off-page, or should this be "a second night"?
10. **Ch3, "the way the jar's last ticket had been right at the bottom."** In Ch1 it was Roz's own name that came from the bottom, and Gus's was the next draw, not the last ticket. Intended?
11. **Ch22, "He thought about it. He thought about it."** I assume the repetition is deliberate (the beat). Please confirm it isn't a doubled line.
12. **Geography.** Roz turns *left* out of the Teague drive "toward Teague Road and home", and Gus drives "a quarter mile east" to his place. Yet Ch20/21 put Roz's porch a quarter mile from Gus's barn ("past the black shape of the Teague barn"), and Ch24 puts it "to the north … a quarter mile off" from the culvert between the two farms. Worth sketching a map so the directions and distances agree.
13. **Ch21, Calumet.** "Bought half this building's inventory at auction last week", but the shop is still full of orange LOT tags and Mitch said earlier the leftovers "go to the auction". Pre-sale or post-sale?
14. **Minor, left alone:** Ch5 "the farm she and Arlo made scary for forty years" (Arlo was alive for 33 of them); Ch8 "he'd had seven hours to dry" (closer to eight); Ch21 Gus said he'd fetch the chest "on a Monday" but goes on a Wednesday; Ch17 "If any of the three of them aren't speaking" (agreement, in dialogue, so left as voice).
