- **Climax is a scene (§5.5).** Yes. The unbelting, the walk down the dock, and the confession happen live (l.5043–5157).
- **No sequel-bait last line (§5.10).** The story's last line is an image. "I hope you'll come back for the next story" (l.5548) is in the back-matter note, not the story.

A low-priority fix: split it, e.g. "Its glass held the window. It was still."
- **Watch item.** The last image is of the retired ghost lantern, not the couple. That is allowed under §5.1 and it is the right thematic close. The couple's last on-page beat is about 60 lines earlier (her palm over **TUES.**, l.5484).
