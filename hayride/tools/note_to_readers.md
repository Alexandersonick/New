## A Note to Readers

Thank you for reading *Two Tickets to the Haunted Hayride*.

If you enjoyed it, the single most helpful thing you can do for an independent author is leave an honest review on Amazon. Reviews, even a line or two, are how new readers find stories like this one, and they mean more than you'd think.

You can also follow me on Amazon: search for Shawn J Dean, open my author page, and tap Follow. You'll hear about new releases first.

Thank you for spending your October with Roz and Gus, and with everybody on the Big Wagon. I hope you'll come back for the next story.

Shawn J Dean
