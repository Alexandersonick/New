## Core structural axes (8)

### 1. Inciting disruption
**What happens:** the heroine has been abroad for 14 weeks with a broken wrist and a facial scar. She comes home a day early without telling anyone. From across the street, in the rain, she watches her twin sister at the husband's dinner for his boss, colleagues and neighbours. The twin is in her chair, in her coat, holding her phone and wearing a drugstore brace that mimics her injury. On the doorstep the twin takes a call from one of the heroine's clients and speaks as her. The boss toasts "To Maud! Finally among us!" [1]. *"My husband stood with everyone else. He lifted his glass toward the near end of the table, where my sister had sat back down in my chair... and he drank, and he sat."*

**Who causes it:**
- **The twin, by commission.** She has passed as the heroine for nine weeks.
- **The husband, by omission and ratification.** On the first night the boss's wife mistook the twin for the heroine. *"I took the corkscrew and opened the bottle and poured."* "It was three seconds." / "It was nine weeks." [2, 3]

**Medium and audience:** in person, at a dinner table, before the husband's boss, colleagues and the whole street. The heroine is the only one who sees it, and she is unseen herself.

**Inciting kind (shape):** `discovery`. It is the discovery of an ongoing betrayal: the twin's commission, ratified by the husband's omission. The first act is a cascade of further discoveries:
- a forged email withdrawing her from a contract [5]
- the twin texting as her [13]
- a forged resignation to her mentor [15]
- her mother as co-author [21]

Function class for scoring: **public erasure by substitution.** Another woman openly holds the wife's place before his people, and he lets it stand.

### 2. The bind
**Stated reason:** she does leave the house at once, so the bind is about why contact continues, not why she stays.
- An email forged in her name cancelled her church-altarpiece contract and returned the deposit [5].
- The certifier who must now sign off believes she has quit the field. Colleagues are warned away: one is in Boston, two have spoken to the mentor and decline [7].
- Her right hand shakes on any fine work.
- The Christmas Eve re-dedication deadline is five weeks away.

**On-page cost:** she has to put the man who poured the wine at her bench every night: *"You owe me nine weeks... You can pay it in evenings."* She also has to supervise him within arm's reach under the certifier's terms [7, 15]. *"This isn't forgiveness."* [7]

### 3. First irreversible decision
- **Act:** on the morning of their sixth anniversary she refuses the explanation ("No.") and works her brass house key off the ring. She sets it beside his still-wrapped gift and walks out to her unheated studio [3]. *"I set the key down beside his badly wrapped parcel, straight, the way he would have set it."*
- **Price:** home, heat, a shower and the anniversary. His spreadsheet "Remediation Plan v1" gets a one-word reply: "No." [4]
- **Chapter:** 3.
- **Follow-on decisions:** [6] she signs a no-deposit, pay-on-certification contract that she cannot perform herself. [7] she dictates the terms that make him her hands.

### 4. Midpoint reversal
**[15] of 30.** The mentor reads the forged resignation aloud in her kitchen: *"I'm done with restoration... Stop sending me articles and stop managing me. I'm not your project."* The mentor had noticed it was off ("honestly", "ceiling", the signature) and believed it anyway, because the "managing" line was true.

**What flips:** the heroine re-frames the theft: *"I had thought... that what my sister took was a house... What she had taken was the name."* She stops arguing her case and hands over the treatment log, written in the husband's capitals. The mentor answers with terms: in the room for every stroke, arm's reach, awake, every shift documented, *"If I find one entry in that book you weren't present for... there will be no certification and no Christmas."*

**Cost:** the whole outcome now rests on the husband never acting unsupervised. That is the trap his core flaw springs in [18]. The midpoint is set up by paired confessions of relief:
- [11] hers: *"It was six weeks somewhere you weren't asking me what I needed... I was glad to go."*
- [12] his: *"I stopped waiting for you around the first week of October."* He then catches himself asking his work-order question: *"Okay. So. What's the ask?"*

### 5. Rupture / lowest point (mechanism)
**First trough [18–19]. The hero's core flaw recurs and causes irreversible harm.**
- She falls asleep after a 14-hour day. He tells the back of her head *"Let me take that off your plate"*, the line he used on her sister in [4].
- He finishes a cleaning area alone and dismisses a yellow swab as "the lamp".
- When his left hand cramps he switches to his untrained right hand, and loses count at forty-one, the same number she stalled at in [9].
- He removes a 13 mm patch of 1886 glaze that cannot be replaced.
- He does not hide it. On graph paper he writes *"CONSERVATOR NOT PRESENT. ASLEEP. NOT TOLD."*, wakes her, and says "Show me."
- Her verdict: *"This is what you do. You make it easier, and it's gone."* [19]

**Second trough [23]. The heroine chooses separation.** A screw backs out of her wrist, so the face can only be done with his hands. She ends the terms because she cannot tell whether she wants him or his usefulness: *"My sister stood where you're standing... and told me that was the happiest she'd ever been, in a house where nobody asked her for anything. I've been living in the same house. I built it."* The chapter ends on the empty phone bowl.

### 6. Climax action
**What:** the night before Christmas Eve, in her studio, he cleans the overpainted Virgin's face. The work is six sections of 90 seconds each, timed between elevated trains, done entirely on her spoken count ("Left. Lift. Stop.") while the certifier watches from a chair [27]. Three decisive beats:
- **Restraint.** At the old damage under the brow her map says LEAVE. He stops "one millimeter short of the edge" and does not tidy it.
- **Self-report.** *"My hand's tired"*, said between sections as the rules require.
- **Steadiness.** He does not move while an early train shakes the table.

The certifier writes: *"Treatment examined 23 December 2026, 11:24 p.m. I certify it. F. Tilley."*

**Caused by:** `joint`.
- **The heroine** asks rather than orders [26: "I'm asking. Not telling."], draws the section map, decided "LEAVE" in advance, and counts.
- **The hero** does the work with restraint.
- **The certifier** makes it binding.
- Her hand shakes throughout; his does not. *"Did he shake?" "No. I did."*

**Staging rhythm:** fail-then-succeed. The relapse comes at [18] and the restraint at [27]. The witnesses vouch for his stillness, not for a grand gesture.

### 8. Resolution & epilogue shape
**Resolution shape:** `renegotiated` reconciliation.
- **Her list of asks** [28]:
  - drive me to the surgeon
  - when I say "nothing", ask once more
  - take me home to sleep, "not to talk"
  - come to the museum and stand before one painting
  - tell me when *you* are tired
  - ask me to your mother's
  - coffee at the place I refused on the first night
- **His one ask:** to keep coming on Saturdays "to be good at something slow".
- **Forgiveness is partial:** *"I'm going to take some of the time. Not all of it."*
- **The sister** gets a March probation: *"Bring nothing."* [21, 29]
- **The mother** is unreconciled. She leaves the Mass at the Lamb of God [29].

**Epilogue device:** [30] is a working Saturday in March, about three months later.
- Two phones are in the bowl.
- He asks "What do you need?" twice, and she answers the second time.
- He is restoring a secret practice painting a friend left him.
- The sister sits on the stool for an hour watching "the slow part" in silence.
- He inspects his cleaned inch and she gives it the verdict "Like nobody was there", a callback to [9].
- She lays a fresh tape square: "Again."

## Supporting structural fields (9–15)

### 9. Central relationship problem
The book asks: *is being unused the same as being loved?* Each spouse was relieved to be unneeded:
- He shields everyone he loves from spending effort, deciding for them and making the repair invisible.
- She stopped asking him for anything in March and was glad to be away.

The sister lived the same thing as the substitute wife and names it: *"That's not being loved," I said. "That's being unused."* [21]. The hero's error is **unilateral helpfulness**: "Let me take that off your plate", "What's the ask?" Its origin is his decision at 11, watching his mother come home exhausted from night shifts [2, 12]: *"nobody I love is going to spend energy they don't have to."*

### 10. Protagonist's dominant strategy
`building`: she controls the relationship through the work and her rules. She uses structured confrontation in set scenes: the kitchen [3], her mother's dinner [14], the sister at the studio [21]. A document trail runs underneath it: voicemails, a friend's screenshot folder, the mentor's printout, the sister's phone.

### 11. Primary opposition mechanism
- **Hidden family authorship.** Her mother's ideology (*"Wives who are present are wives who are kept"*) is carried out through the sister. The mother edits the forged resignation by text: *"Make it sound like her. Sharper."* … *"Better."* [21]
- **An impersonal institutional gate.** The commission needs an independent certifier, by a fixed liturgical deadline [6, 22].
- **The hero's own flaw.** It is the internal antagonist that produces the lowest point.

### 12. Secret / information architecture
A layered cascade. The heroine knows first, and the others learn that she knows.

| Ch | Who knows | What surfaces |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Heroine only, unseen | The substitution |
| 3 | Husband and sister | That she saw. The reader learns it started Sept 11, as a mistaken greeting ratified by the husband |
| 5 | Heroine | A voicemail reveals a withdrawal email sent in her name and $11,400 returned. The husband did not know |
| 13 | Heroine | A friend's screenshot folder shows the sister texting as her, and hints the mother knew ("she's right for once") |
| 14 | Heroine | The mother's tells: she smells the sister's gardenia on the coat, and says "your health or your painting" before being told |
| 15 | Heroine | The mentor reads the resignation aloud |
| 16 | Hero | The sister admits the email to him |
| 21 | Heroine | The sister's phone proves the mother co-wrote it. The sister's motive: *"Because I wanted to stay... It was the house."* |

**Hero's secrets:**
- He stopped waiting for her in October. He confesses it [12].
- The promotion offer is hidden [10–20] and then declined. The boss's wife reveals it, *"Because he won't."* [25]

**Inverted secret:** he refuses to hide his damage [18]. She refuses to remove the page from the record: *"It isn't the point of a hinge to take things out when nobody's looking."* [19]

**Contraband:** a friend secretly gives him a practice painting [24]. The heroine finds it and pretends not to know [30].

### 13. Secondary plot engine and collision
**Engine:** a church-altarpiece restoration on a certification deadline. The overpaint hides the original face, and a sign painter's careful 1957 job was laid over damage "so that nobody would have to look at the damage" [6]. That is the hero's flaw rendered as an object.

**Collisions:**
- **It creates the containment.** She needs his hands [7].
- **It turns the midpoint.** Certification depends on the log [15].
- **It converts his relapse into a permanent visible record.** UV makes any retouch read dark forever [19].
- **It forces the final choice.** After the screw fails, the face cannot be done without him [23].

**Tertiary engines:**
- His promotion deadline (Dec 18) collides with "evenings" [10, 20].
- The sister's accountability list (11 names by Dec 8) moves the public correction off the hero and onto the person who did it [9, 21].

### 15. What the job and the setting make possible
**Conservation** supplies the logic of the plot, not just its texture:
- **Layered overpaint over an original** mirrors the substitution of one face for another.
- **Reversibility and visible retouch** make repair honest rather than invisible: *"I won't pretend there's glaze where there isn't."* The hero's mistake becomes a permanent, UV-readable mark.
- **The operator log with initials** turns a confession into evidence the certifier trusts: *"A liar doesn't hinge a page like that into the book."* [23]
- **The heroine's injured hand** forces the dependency.
- **The climax is a craft judgment:** "Leave it", the decision not to tidy old damage.

**The hero's job** (regional freight-network operations) makes possible:
- the sequencing and efficiency flaw ("Okay. So. One: ...")
- the boss and colleagues as the deceived audience
- the promotion-versus-evenings test
- steady hands and dock-form capitals that make the log legible

**The studio** (a cold former factory, with a landlord and neighbours) is the heroine's own territory and runs on her rules: phone bowl, no food, Sundays alone. Trains every 11 minutes and a tap dripping every 4 seconds set the clock of the climax.

**Occupation-swap test (H3):**
- Axis 1 (the substitution) survives a swap.
- Axis 4 (forgery to a mentor) partly survives.
- Axes 6 and 7 (a craft act of restraint under an examiner, and repair as apprenticeship producing a visible record) do not survive. The profession is causal.
