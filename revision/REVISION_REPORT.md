## What changed, by finding

**R01: repeated restoration instruction (high).** Cut the unchanged recaps and kept every beat that changes something:
- Ch 8: removed the swab-change Q&A and the second dictation. Tightened the surface-dirt explanation and one instruction.
- Ch 9 and Ch 17: the full operator formula is summarized instead of recited again.
- Ch 12: the September 11 wine scene, already dramatized in Ch 2 and Ch 3, is replayed in two sentences. The Valley Forge confession now lands once, in the scene with Maud, instead of being narrated and then repeated aloud.
- Ch 15: Frances reads one log entry, not three, and the rule recap is shortened.
- Ch 19: removed the restated "why it darkens" exchange.
- Ch 26: the six-section list is not repeated, because Ch 25's Mylar scene already gives it.
- Ch 27: Frances's log reading now goes straight to the hinge, and one unchanged count is cut.

**R02: explained symbolism (high).**
- Ch 17: the portrait's old damage no longer mirrors Maud's scar exactly. It is now a ~6 mm break at the outer end of the brow under the veil, not a 2 cm wound into the hairline. This was the independent reader's main hypothetical DNF point. Ch 25–27 were updated to match.
- Ch 23: the useful-hands diagnosis is said aloud once, not narrated and then said.
- Ch 25: Atlanta is now named on the page as "the corner again, done kindly", which removes the risk of reading the secret sacrifice as endorsed. The restated self-diagnosis is cut.
- Ch 27: "Whose voice? / Mine. / Then it's yours" is cut. The scene keeps "Whose hands? / His." and lets the reader finish it.

**R03: reciprocal choice (medium).**
- Ch 28: "Next time I'll tell you, and *we* decide it." Declan asks to keep coming on Saturdays to *learn* the work, not to be her hands. "The nine weeks are paid" becomes "The evenings were never going to pay for the nine weeks. I've stopped wanting them to."
- Ch 30: new exchange. Declan wants both days of Easter in Scranton, which clashes with Maud's promise to Mrs. Tsang. They negotiate.

**R04: verification plausibility (medium).** Ch 5, in a live scene before any confession: Odalys says she called Pauline in October and was told Maud was sleeping 16 hours a day, wanted no calls, and had asked to keep her from flying over. That is why Odalys closed her first ticket. This also plants Pauline's complicity early. Ch 13 was updated to say the second ticket followed the photo.

**R05: conservation scope (medium, still needs specialist review).** Ch 6 now states that the five weeks cover phase one only: consolidation, cleaning, and the overpaint off the face. Lining and retouching move to the summer, and the contract wording matches. The treatment method, its speed and the novice work on the face have **not** been checked by a paintings conservator. Do that before publication.

**R06: the absolute in Ch 23.** "He has done exactly what I said for four weeks" now names the exception she hinged into the book.

**Timetable.** Ch 23 and Ch 25 now make the release a deliberate professional risk with a stated deadline: decide by the 23rd, or call Frances off and let Wrobel's face hang for one more Christmas.

**Follow-up after the hostile reader's PASS (low-severity items).**
- "never once" cut from 26 uses to 11 (rank-11 voice tic).
- Removed one residual gloss in Ch 23 and one in Ch 27.

**Reader note (back matter).** Asks for an honest Amazon review and says how to follow the author ("search for Shawn J Dean, open my author page, and tap Follow"). There are **no links** anywhere in the DOCX, PDF or EPUB.
